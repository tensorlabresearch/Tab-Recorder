Filter Syntax

Plain text — substring match on title, description, and transcript.

title.contains("text") — case-insensitive match on title/description.

title.matches("regex") — regex match on title/description.

transcript.contains("text") — match within transcript text.

transcript.matches("regex") — regex match on transcript.

date.after("2026-01-01") — recordings from after that date.

date.before("2026-01-31") — recordings from before that date.

date.range("2026-01-01","2026-01-31") — inclusive start, exclusive end.

has("transcript") / has("mp3") — filter by artifact presence.

Combine with && (AND), || (OR), and parentheses.

Example: title.contains("standup") && date.range("2026-06-01","2026-06-30")
